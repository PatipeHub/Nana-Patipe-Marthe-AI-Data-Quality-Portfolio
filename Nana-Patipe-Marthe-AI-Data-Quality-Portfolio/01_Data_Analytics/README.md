# Superstore — Sales & Profitability Analysis

## Project Overview
This portfolio project analyzes Superstore sales and profitability using **Excel and Microsoft Power BI**.

The objective is to identify sales trends, profitable and loss-making products, the role of discounts, and actionable business recommendations.

## Objectives
- Monitor sales and profitability.
- Analyze sales trends over time.
- Identify the most and least profitable products.
- Examine discounts and their relationship with profitability.
- Translate findings into business recommendations.

## Tools
- Microsoft Excel
- Microsoft Power BI
- DAX

## Dashboard
### Executive Overview
- Total Sales: **2.33M**
- Total Profit: **292.30K**
- Profit Margin: **12.56%**
- Average Discount: **15.54%**

### Product Analysis
The dashboard compares products using Total Sales, Total Profit, Profit Margin % and Average Discount.

### Top 10 Loss-Making Products
The Top 10 products with the lowest Total Profit generate approximately **-29.46K** in combined losses.

The three most loss-making products account for approximately **-17.31K**, or nearly **59%** of the Top 10 losses.

The largest loss is recorded by **Cubify CubeX 3D Printer Double Head Print** at approximately **-8.88K**, with a **-80% profit margin**.

Several highly unprofitable products have average discounts of **40% or more**. This is a warning signal that the discount policy should be investigated as a possible contributor to margin erosion.

## Key Business Insights
1. Sales growth does not necessarily mean profit growth.
2. A small group of products concentrates a large share of the losses.
3. High discounts frequently appear among highly unprofitable products.
4. Profit and Profit Margin should be monitored alongside Sales.

## Recommendations
1. **Review loss-making products** — reassess prices, costs and commercial conditions.
2. **Control high discounts** — establish discount thresholds based on minimum acceptable margins.
3. **Prioritize profitable products** — identify and develop products with strong margins.
4. **Monitor profitability continuously** — use Profit and Margin alongside Sales.
