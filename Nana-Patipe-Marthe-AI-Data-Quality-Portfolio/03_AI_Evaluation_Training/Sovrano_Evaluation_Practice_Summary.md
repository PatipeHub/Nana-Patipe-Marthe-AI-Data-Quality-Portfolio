# AI Evaluation & Human Feedback — Practice Summary

## Scope
Independent learning and practice focused on AI response evaluation, preference comparison, guideline adherence, and evidence-based judgment.

## Evaluation Foundations
Topics covered include:
- RLHF and preference-based evaluation
- Human judgment and reward signals
- Goodhart's Law and proxy optimization
- Sycophancy, length/formatting bias, reward hacking, and confident hallucination
- Guideline-first evaluation
- Defensible preference judgments and concise rationales
- Accuracy, relevance, safety, honesty, clarity, and instruction following
- Ambiguity handling and escalation rather than guessing

## Preference Comparison Practice
Practice involved comparing Response A vs Response B and selecting the stronger response based on the rubric rather than surface characteristics such as length, formatting, confidence, or agreement with the user.

## Domain Practice
Preference/evaluation exercises included business-oriented scenarios such as:
- Business Strategy
- Corporate Finance & Valuation
- Marketing & Go-to-Market
- Operations & Supply Chain

## Quality Principles
1. Read the task guideline completely.
2. Apply ranked criteria consistently.
3. Prioritize correctness, relevance, safety, and instruction following over style.
4. Give a short rationale explaining the deciding factor.
5. Separate genuine ambiguity from evaluator error.
6. Avoid unsupported assumptions.

Practice-based independent learning. This document is not presented as paid professional AI evaluation employment.
